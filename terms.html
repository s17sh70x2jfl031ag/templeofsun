6. Essential oils: important safety information

Essential oils are highly concentrated plant substances. They deserve respect and careful handling. For every blend we sell or recommend:

  • External use only. Never swallow essential oils or use them internally, and never use undiluted oils on the skin unless the product is expressly pre-diluted for that purpose.
  • Dilute before skin use as instructed on each product, and do a small patch test on the inner forearm before wider use. Discontinue immediately if redness, itching or irritation appears.
  • Keep away from children and pets, away from eyes, ears and mucous membranes, and store closed at room temperature away from sunlight.
  • Pregnancy, breastfeeding, epilepsy, medication. If any of these apply to you, or you are under a doctor's care, consult a physician before using any blend. Certain oils are not suitable in these situations.
  • Photosensitivity. Some citrus-based blends can make skin sensitive to light; avoid direct sun and UV light for 8 to 12 hours after applying such blends to the skin.
  • Follow each product's own label and cautions, which form part of these terms. Recommended usage rhythms, such as rest weeks after continuous use, exist for your safety.
  • Allergies and sensitive skin. Blends contain natural plant, nut, seed and fruit derived substances that can cause sensitivities. Review each ingredient list before use; every person is different and reactions can never be fully predicted. If you have known allergies or very sensitive skin, ask us before purchasing.
  • Animals. Essential oils can be harmful to pets, cats especially. Keep bottles out of their reach and be considerate when diffusing in rooms shared with animals.
  • Flammability. Essential oils are flammable. Keep them away from open flame, candles and heat sources.

We accept no responsibility for reactions or damage arising from use of the products contrary to their instructions and cautions, or from failure to disclose relevant conditions.
