Meditation Guidance

rediscover clarity, balance, and inner peace through personalised practice

Perhaps this sounds like you

When the practice keeps slipping

Do you often feel mentally or emotionally exhausted? Do you find it difficult to build, or return to, a consistent meditation practice? Are you longing for guidance to help you meditate with ease and confidence?

If so, this one-to-one programme is designed especially for you. Over the course of one week we meet online for 40 minutes each day, where I personally guide you through foundational meditation techniques rooted in Tibetan Buddhist practice, complemented by Pranayama breathing exercises.

Each session is adapted to your current level and needs, helping you establish a stable and personalised meditation routine that feels natural and sustainable, without pressure or disappointment.

What the week is for

Something you look forward to

Throughout the week I hold a safe and supportive space for you to explore different approaches to meditation and experience their effects on your body, mind and emotions. My intention is to help you develop confidence and connection with your own inner stillness, so your practice becomes something you genuinely look forward to.

Where this comes from

Since I was a child

Meditation has been part of my life since childhood. In the beginning it served as a coping mechanism, an escape into the spiritual world. My first formal experience came during my initial Reiki training at age 15, and from that point onward meditation gradually evolved into a practice of acceptance, awareness and inner spaciousness.

Over the past decade I have studied and practised Vipassana meditation, in both the Goenka and Mahasi traditions, as well as Tibetan Buddhist pranayama and meditation at dedicated centres. A two-year journey through India and Southeast Asia deepened this path of exploration and self-discovery.

“It would be my pleasure and honour to share the insights, tools, and practices that have supported me along the way, so that you, too, can cultivate peace, focus, and inner balance in your everyday life.”
