Mindfulness LABs

alchemical journeys, taken together

What a LAB is · in my own words

A living workshop

Over the years, I have developed a series of holistic workshops that I lovingly call “Meditation LABs.” These immersive experiences bring together a rich blend of tools and techniques I have worked with for many years, carefully designed to support collective transformation and inner exploration within a group setting.

Aromatherapy alchemy, pranayama breathwork, Kriya and guided meditation, chanting, journaling, chakra exercises, sound healing, visualisation and oracle readings: each concept fluid by design, evolving with the astrological energies and the dynamics of the group, so no two are ever the same.

These workshops are an invitation to experiment, to awaken the senses, and to reconnect with the sacred space within, where transformation begins.

In May 2026 I brought a Mindfulness LAB to ELLE Yoga Weekend – Ride the Fire in Budapest, invited by ELLE Hungary: igniting the inner creative fire.

The five LABs

A curated series of immersive group experiences designed to restore balance, deepen awareness and elevate the senses. Each session blends breath, scent, sound and guided practice into a refined and intuitive journey, shaped by the energy of the group.

Homecoming

“I’m busy, I’m meeting myself”

Known as “yogic sleep”, this guided meditation gently leads you into the state between waking and sleeping. A deeply restorative practice that resets the nervous system and invites profound physical, mental and emotional release.

Yoga Nidra · 50 minutes · no experience needed

Anahata

“I’m busy, I’m practising love”

A soft yet powerful journey into the heart space. Mindful breath, subtle body awareness and guided loving-kindness meditation (Metta Bhavana) invite emotional release, inner openness and a deeper connection to compassion, for yourself and for others.

Heart Opening Meditation · 50 minutes · no experience needed

Prana

“I’m busy, I’m breathing”

Ancient breathwork practices from Indian and Tibetan traditions, brought into a modern, accessible experience. Through guided techniques you regulate the nervous system, increase vitality and deepen present-moment awareness.

Pranayama · 50 minutes · no experience needed

Connect

“I’m busy, I’m exploring the elements”

Inspired by Vipassana, energy body awareness and contemplative traditions, this guided session offers practical tools to cultivate inner stillness, clarity and presence. Suitable for beginners and experienced practitioners alike.

Meditation · 50 minutes · no experience needed

Alchemy

“I’m busy, I’m taking a soul-bath”

An immersive sensory journey into the world of essential oils. You explore aromatic profiles, therapeutic properties and their subtle effects through guided inhalation and mindful application.

Aromatherapy Workshop · 50 minutes · no experience needed

Custom LAB concepts are also designed for retreats, collaborations and special gatherings, tailored to the theme, intention and energy of the invitation.

For venues & events

Classes for your guests

For hotels, resorts and beach clubs, any of the five LABs is held as a 50-minute group class. They work best outdoors or in a calm, quiet room, and no prior experience is needed.
