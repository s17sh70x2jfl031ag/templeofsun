One hour · One time · Online

Holistic Health Consultation

receive personalised guidance designed to restore harmony from within

In our 60-minute session I guide you through a health and lifestyle questionnaire to explore your unique needs. Together we look at your current challenges, habitual patterns, areas of imbalance, your lifestyle and daily routines.

Based on your elemental and Doshic constitution, I offer practical recommendations (new directions, foods, habits and tools) that support your everyday harmony and wellbeing. We also discuss your health history, emotional landscape and mental patterns, and from this holistic picture I create personalised aromatherapy suggestions to assist you.

If you wish, ongoing aromatherapy support continues afterwards, wherever you live.

Three months · Six sessions + intro call · Online

Spiritual Mentoring & Transformation

reconnect with your authentic self through aromatherapy, empathy and presence

A transformative three-month, one-on-one journey for personal growth, healing and alignment. We meet online twice a month for 60 minutes (six sessions plus an introduction call) and you receive three + one templeofsun aromatherapy formulas of 5ml each, specially created for your needs and the themes we explore. Each month you work with one formula daily; the additional blend supports your integration at the end.

Over more than a decade I have witnessed the profound healing power of aromatherapy, and how it opens pathways toward balance. Combined with mindful mentoring and active listening, it can catalyse life-changing shifts. Along the way you also receive personalised meditation practices, reflective exercises and holistic tools that nurture the specific parts of your health and life we are tending.

“I understand how intimate and personal this kind of work is. I approach every session with empathy, presence, and dedication. Throughout our time together, I will be fully here for you: offering a safe, supportive, and nurturing space for your transformation to unfold.”

One week · 40 minutes daily · Online

Meditation Guidance

rediscover clarity, balance, and inner peace through personalised practice

Do you often feel mentally or emotionally exhausted? Do you find it difficult to build, or return to, a consistent meditation practice? Are you longing for guidance to help you meditate with ease and confidence?

If so, this one-on-one program is for you. For one week we meet online, 40 minutes a day. I personally guide you through foundational meditation techniques rooted in Tibetan Buddhist practice, complemented by Pranayama breathing exercises. Each session adapts to your current level and needs, so your routine feels natural and sustainable, built without pressure or disappointment.

Through the week you explore different approaches and feel their effects on body, mind and emotions, developing the confidence and connection with inner stillness that makes you genuinely look forward to your practice.

“It would be my pleasure and honour to share the insights, tools, and practices that have supported me along the way, so that you, too, can cultivate peace, focus, and inner balance in your everyday life.”
