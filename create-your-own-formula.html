Aromatherapy · Bespoke

Create Your Own Formula

a signature scent for your space

The idea · in Peter's words

Scent is memory

“I’m beyond excited about our collaboration of creating your personalised alchemy formula, for you to spread the magic of aromatherapy within your circle.

Your unique personalised blend will naturally encompass your intentions, your view of life, your philosophy, your ideology, and your love towards everyone you share it with.

As a yoga teacher, space holder, therapist, business owner or hotel manager, you have the power and the opportunity to enhance the energy of your surroundings by using aromatherapy purposefully in your space.

Using your formula will help your students, guests, colleagues and clients to feel safe and at ease in your space. It will naturally support the integration of their day, of your teachings, of the experience you provide: helping them feel more harmonious, inspired and supported, or simply giving them a sense of belonging.

You only need to diffuse your formula around your people. The scent itself will remind them — the sense of smell is connected to memories — of the feeling they had during your class, during your retreat, or during their stay in your hotel or café, whenever they use it at home by themselves. It immediately creates an invisible bond between you and your people.

Unconsciously, after a few times of using it, it gives them a sense of community every time they inhale it. So they will want to return to your class, your retreat, your café or your office, and experience that pleasant energy all over again. Having your own personalised formula and sharing it will eventually create the feeling of togetherness and community.”

with love, Peter

The process

How it works

01 · Presence

We tune in

A conversation about your space, your people and the feeling you want to hold: calm, welcome, focus, celebration.

02 · Alchemy

Blended with intention

Peter composes your formula from 100% pure, laboratory-tested oils, blended, bottled and blessed in a meditative state.

03 · Ritual

Your scent, your ritual

You receive your signature formula to diffuse and share, with guidance on how to weave it into your space and moments.

Who it's for

Spaces that hold people

Yoga teachers, space holders, teachers, therapists, business owners, hotel managers. Boutique hotels, retreat venues, cafés, studios and offices. Anyone whose work is a feeling, and who wants that feeling remembered.

Not to mention that aromatherapy, being one of the most powerful plant medicines because of its high concentration, will support everyone's wellbeing. Aromatherapy is a complementary medicine. It instantly raises the frequency of the energy body, gives a huge amount of emotional support, and has the ability to help people heal themselves on all levels: body, mind and soul.
