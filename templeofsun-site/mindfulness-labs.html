What a LAB is · in my own words

A living workshop

Over the years, I have developed a series of holistic workshops that I lovingly call “Meditation LABs.” These immersive experiences bring together a rich blend of tools and techniques I have worked with for many years, carefully designed to support collective transformation and inner exploration within a group setting.

Aromatherapy alchemy, pranayama breathwork, Kriya and guided meditation, chanting, journaling, chakra exercises, sound healing, visualisation and oracle readings: each concept fluid by design, evolving with the astrological energies and the dynamics of the group, so no two are ever the same.

These workshops are an invitation to experiment, to awaken the senses, and to reconnect with the sacred space within, where transformation begins.

For venues & events

Classes for your guests

For hotels, resorts and beach clubs, the LABs distil into a menu of 50-minute group classes: small circles of up to twelve, held in a calm space, no experience needed:

  • Yoga Nidra
    guided “yogic sleep”: conscious deep rest for the nervous system
  • Meditation
    Vipassana and mindfulness tools for stillness and clarity
  • Sound Healing
    Tibetan bowls, voice and vibration, felt at a cellular level
  • Pranayama
    ancient breath science to regulate, restore and enliven
  • Aromatherapy Workshop
    an experiential journey into essential oils and their gifts
