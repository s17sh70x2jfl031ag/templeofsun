The idea · in Peter's words

Scent is memory

“I'm beyond excited about our collaboration: creating your personalised alchemy formula, for you to spread the magic of aromatherapy within your circle.

Your blend will naturally carry your intentions, your view of life, your philosophy and your love, everything you already give to the people in your space.

Diffuse it around your people, and the scent does quiet work: your students, guests and clients feel safe and at ease, the day's experience integrates more deeply, and every breath offers a sense of belonging.

And because smell is wired to memory, one breath of it at home brings back the feeling of your class, your retreat, your café. An invisible bond that slowly grows into community, and brings your people back to you.”

with love, Peter

The process

How it works

01 · Presence

A conversation about your space, your people and the feeling you want to hold: calm, welcome, focus, celebration.

02 · Alchemy

Blended with intention

Peter composes your formula from 100% pure, laboratory-tested oils, blended, bottled and blessed in a meditative state.
