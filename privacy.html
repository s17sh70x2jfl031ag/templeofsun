Privacy Policy

what you share with us stays between us

Last updated: 4 August 2026

1. Who is responsible for your data

The controller of personal data collected through this website and our services is Péter Frák, trading as Temple of Sun (templeofsun), practising primarily in Portugal and Hungary. For anything related to your personal data, write to templeofsunofficial@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

We collect only what you choose to give us:

  • Contact form and email. Your name, email address, chosen topic and the message you write.
  • Newsletter and ebook. Your email address, when you subscribe or request the free ebook.
  • Bookings and sessions. Contact details and the practical information needed to arrange a treatment, class, online session or retreat, such as dates and location preferences.
  • Health information you volunteer. To keep treatments and retreats safe, we may ask about relevant conditions such as pregnancy, epilepsy, allergies or medication. Sharing this is your choice; it is treated as sensitive data with special care, as described in section 5.
  • Messaging platforms. If you contact us on WhatsApp, Telegram or Instagram, we receive what you send there, under those platforms' own terms.

3. What we do not collect

  • We use no advertising or tracking cookies and no analytics profiles on this website.
  • The Find Your Blend questionnaire runs entirely in your own browser. Your answers are stored only temporarily on your device so your results survive page changes, and they are never transmitted to us.
  • We do not buy, sell or trade personal data, ever.

4. Why we use your data, and on what legal basis

  • Answering your messages and providing information you asked for: our legitimate interest in responding, and steps taken at your request before entering a contract (GDPR art. 6(1)(b) and (f)).
  • Providing booked services, including treatments, sessions, classes and retreats: performance of a contract (art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Newsletter and ebook. Your consent (art. 6(1)(a)), which you may withdraw at any time using the unsubscribe option in any letter or by writing to us.
  • Safety-related health information. Your explicit consent (art. 9(2)(a)); see section 5.
  • Legal obligations, such as accounting records for purchases (art. 6(1)(c)).

5. Health information, held with special care

Details you share about your body and health exist for one purpose only: your safety and the personalisation of your session, blend or retreat participation. They are shared with no one, used for nothing else, kept no longer than needed for your care and any legal retention periods, and deleted on request where the law allows. You may decline to share health details, in which case we may need to adapt or decline certain services for your safety. Everything you share in sessions is held in confidence.

6. Who we share data with

Only with service providers who make our work possible, and only the minimum they need:

  • our email provider, to receive and answer your messages;
  • a newsletter delivery service, if you subscribe;
  • payment providers, if you purchase, who process your payment under their own terms;
  • authorities, only where the law requires it.

Retreat venues receive only the practical information needed for your stay, never your session content.

7. Embedded content and browser storage

  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts; when your browser fetches them, Google receives your IP address, under Google's privacy policy.
  • Images and videos on some pages load from our media hosting (including the wp.com content network), which technically receives your IP address to deliver the files.
  • Browser storage is used only for the Find Your Blend results on your own device, as described in section 3. No identifier is attached and nothing leaves your browser.
  • Outbound links to WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, TikTok and Spotify lead to services with their own privacy policies.

8. Where your data lives

We work within the European Union wherever possible. Some providers, such as email, font or media services, may process data outside the EU; where that happens, it is done under recognised safeguards such as the EU standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision.

9. How long we keep it

  • Correspondence: as long as our conversation and any follow-up care require, then deleted in periodic reviews.
  • Newsletter data: until you unsubscribe.
  • Booking and purchase records: for the retention periods required by tax and accounting law.
  • Safety-related health notes: only while relevant to your care, unless a longer period is legally required.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you may, at any time: request access to the data we hold about you; have it corrected or completed; have it erased; restrict or object to its processing; receive it in a portable format; and withdraw any consent, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal. Write to us and we will respond within one month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular the CNPD in Portugal (cnpd.pt), the NAIH in Hungary (naih.hu), or the authority of your own country of residence.

11. Children

This website and our services are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16; if you believe a child has sent us personal data, write to us and we will delete it.

12. How we protect it

We keep the data we hold in access-protected accounts and devices, share it with no one beyond section 6, and collect so little that there is very little to protect. No internet transmission is ever perfectly secure, which is one more reason we ask you to share only what is needed.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time; the version published here, with its date, is the current one. Meaningful changes will be visible on this page.

14. Contact

For anything about your data, or to exercise any right in section 10, write to templeofsunofficial@gmail.com or use the contact page. See also our Terms of Use.

In short, and with love: we collect almost nothing, we track nothing, your quiz answers never leave your device, and what you choose to share with Peter stays with Peter.
