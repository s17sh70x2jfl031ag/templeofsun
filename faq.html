Oils & blends

What exactly is a templeofsun blend?

Each blend is a formula of pure essential oils that I compose and blend by hand, in small batches. Every formula is built around a feeling or a moment of life rather than around a single scent. On each blend's page you will find the exact plants inside it and the story of why they are together.

How do I choose the right blend for me?

Start with the feeling, not the scent. Ask yourself what you need more of right now: sleep, focus, courage, grounding, letting go. The Find Your Blend questionnaire walks you there in a few gentle questions, or you can wander the Rainbow Collection and see which name calls you. Your first instinct is usually right.

What is the Rainbow Collection?

Eighteen blends, each made around a feeling. The collection was born in 2020, after a twelve-hour New Year's Eve ceremony in an ashram in India, and took shape over a year of quiet refining in the Portuguese mountains. The signature Rainbow blend itself holds 74 essential oils in one bottle. The full story lives on the collection page.

What are the 5 Elements formulas?

Ayurveda teaches that all matter is made of five elements, the Panchamahabhuta: earth, water, fire, air and ether. The 5 Elements collection is five holistic formulas, one for each element, made to balance an excess or a deficiency of that element within you. The page includes a map of the elements and chakras to guide you.

How do you make sure the oils you use are genuinely pure?

I choose only oils labelled as pure essential oil, never the cheaper “aromatherapy oil” blends stretched with filler carriers. Real rose, jasmine and neroli are costly to extract, so a very cheap bottle is telling you something. I keep them in dark glass, away from light, and choose cold-pressed, virgin base oils. More on what quality looks like is on the aromatherapy page.

Is there a ritual to how the blends are made?

Yes. Every templeofsun formula is composed in a meditative state, with prayer, rather than mixed like a product on a line. You can read more about the path behind the blends in my story.

What is the Jivamukti Essence?

It is a twelve-oil formula I created for Jivamukti Yoga, born from Anahata treatments I gave their teachers on the island of Crete in February 2022. The blend is light blue-green, representing Anahata, the heart chakra, and comes as a concentrated essential oil or as a gentler roll-on natural perfume in jojoba base oil. It is sold directly through Jivamukti's own shops, not through templeofsun. The full story and safety notes (not for use during pregnancy or in case of epilepsy, and phototoxic, so keep treated skin out of the sun for 8 hours) are on the Jivamukti Collaboration page.

Using them safely

How do I use a blend day to day?

Slowly, and with attention. Breathe the blend in for a few calm breaths, let it touch the skin where the pulse lives, and give the moment a little silence. The oils work best when they are part of a small ritual rather than a rushed habit. Each blend's page suggests its own ritual.

Can essential oils go directly on my skin?

Never undiluted. Essential oils are concentrated plant power, and the rule I hold to is simple: dilute before skin, in a good carrier oil, staying at or under a 2.5 percent dilution unless a professional guides you otherwise. Each of my blends comes with its own ritual of use on its page. If your skin is sensitive, test a small patch on the inner forearm first and wait a day. Keep oils away from the eyes and from broken skin, and if anything feels wrong, stop and write to me.

I am pregnant, or I have a health condition. Can I still use the oils?

Some plants are wonderful friends in pregnancy and some are better left for later, and the same is true for conditions like epilepsy, asthma or ongoing medication. Please tell me before you choose a blend or book a treatment, and I will guide you honestly, including telling you when the answer is no. Your safety always comes before a sale.

About me

Who is behind Temple of Sun?

I am Péter Frák, an aromatherapist and holistic practitioner from Hungary, living between Portugal and Hungary. templeofsun is my life's work: the blends, the treatments, the retreats and everything on this site come from my own hands. The short version of my path is on the bio page, the long one in my story.

What is your training?

I am a member of the International Federation of Aromatherapists, hold a two-year aromatherapy diploma from Panarom in Budapest, and am certified in Ayurvedic and Marma-Abhyanga massage by the Mysore Ayurveda Academy in India. The full list of my credentials is on the bio page.

What is your experience hosting retreats?

Before hosting my own, I spent four seasons as in-house therapist, co-facilitator and meditation guide at Vale de Moses, one of Europe's most loved yoga retreats in the Portuguese mountains, supporting more than fifty retreats and massaging hundreds of guests.
