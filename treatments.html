Therapies · treatments · healing

First, I tune in to you

Every treatment starts the same way: not with a menu, but with a moment of listening.

Before we begin, I tune in to who is in front of me. I ask what you need in that moment, and only then do I gather the aromas for your journey. No two sessions are the same, because no two people, and no two days, are. My guests describe feeling safe and seen, then leaving energized and grounded, often with a custom blend to carry the work home.

Signature · 90 minutes

Anahata

mind bending, heart opening holistic therapy

I created Anahata during the pandemic, when hopelessness and separation felt sharpest, when so many of us lost touch with our heart space. This is my answer: a treatment built to reconnect you with your heart, support emotional healing, and let held grief and sadness finally move.

It is a multidimensional, multisensory experience that is difficult to fully explain; the mind struggles to grasp the depth of the process. Through personalised aromatherapy formulas, guided meditation, sound healing, multidimensional energy work, the power of loving touch, prayers and angel cards, we tend inner-child wounds, balance the chakra system, and plant energetic seeds for change.

Ayurvedic · 60 or 80 minutes

Marma-Abhyanga

Ayurvedic full body anointing massage therapy

A rhythmic application of warm herbal oils to the skin, muscles and connective tissues, with focused stimulation of the Marma points, vital energy centres throughout the body. Ayurvedic tradition holds that regular practice enhances the flow of prana, improves circulation, strengthens resilience to stress, supports immune function, calms the nervous system, relieves tension and improves sleep.

My version blends the tradition with lymphatic and deep-tissue technique and my own art of loving touch, on a regular massage bed rather than the traditional wooden table. I choose your oils from my aromatherapy-alchemy studies, personalised to your constitution: a sacred journey of rejuvenation, relaxation and reconnection to the self.
